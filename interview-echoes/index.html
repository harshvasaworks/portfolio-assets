Interviewer: Walk me through how you would decide what ships first on a roadmap like this one.

Candidate: I would start from the customer problem, size the impact against the effort, and then pressure-test it with the team before committing.

Interviewer: And where does AI fit into that process for you day to day?

Candidate: Mostly summarising research and drafting specs, one prompt at a time. It saves hours every week.

Interviewer: Do you check what it hands back before it goes anywhere?

Candidate: I skim it, yes, but I do not have a formal review step. If something looks off I rewrite that part by hand.

Interviewer: Tell me about a time a stakeholder disagreed with your prioritisation.

Candidate: We had two enterprise accounts pulling in opposite directions, so I brought both into one working session and made the trade-offs visible.

Interviewer: What did you measure afterwards to know the decision was right?

Candidate: Activation and retention for the cohort, plus a follow-up with both accounts a quarter later.
